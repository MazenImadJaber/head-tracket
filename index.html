24-hour factor review

This compares recorded headache starts with explicitly logged no-headache check-ins. Only answered Yes/No questions contribute. Unknowns and old v5 factors are excluded.

Small samples, recall bias, overlapping factors and missing no-headache days can distort patterns. This is descriptive, not a test for migraine or proof of causation.

Your data

No account

Your entries are saved in this app’s browser storage on this iPhone. The app does not send symptom data to a server. Removing the app, clearing website data or changing devices may erase your history.

GP report (PDF)

Create a local 30- or 90-day clinician summary of headache features, reported 24-hour factors and gaps in the diary. Nothing is uploaded.

Check-in reminders

Optional reminders appear when this app is open or reopened. iPhone web apps cannot schedule reliable background alerts without a push-notification service; this version uses no server.

Back up and export

Export JSON regularly to keep a restorable backup. CSV is useful for spreadsheets or sharing a diary with a clinician.

Upload / restore a backup

Select a HeadTrack JSON backup from Files or iCloud Drive. The file is processed on your device; nothing is uploaded to a server.

You will review a confirmation before importing. All entries, including episodes, are merged by ID; matching IDs in the backup replace local records. Your custom factors are also restored. Older backups are supported.
